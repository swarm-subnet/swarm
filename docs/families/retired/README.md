# Retired families

These challenge families take no submissions, are not scored and pay nothing. Swarm Sentinel replaced all three in one release, and its slice of emissions is the sum of theirs. Their guides stay here for reference, and every past champion stays published in the [champions repository](https://github.com/swarm-subnet/swarm-champions).

| Family | ID | Maps it ran on | Share it carried |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Autopilot](autopilot.md) | `cf_autopilot` | City, Open, Mountain, Village, Warehouse, Forest | 15% |
| [Swarm Autopilot](swarm_autopilot.md) | `cf_swarm_autopilot` | City, Open, Mountain, Village, Forest | 20% |
| [Office Interceptor](interceptor_office.md) | `cf_interceptor_office` | Office (challenge type 7) | 30% |

The office map (challenge type 7) was used only by Office Interceptor, so it retired with it. The other maps live on in the rescue families.

The live families are [Swarm Sentinel](../solar_patrol.md), [Swarm Search and Rescue](../swarm_sar.md) and [Search and Rescue](../search_and_rescue.md). [Interceptor](../interceptor.md) is a different case: its champion solved it, so it is completed rather than retired.

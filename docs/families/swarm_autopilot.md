# Swarm Autopilot

One policy, up to eight drones, one shared pool of landing pads.

Swarm Autopilot (`cf_swarm_autopilot`) is the team version of Autopilot: each seed spawns **2–8 drones**, all flown by one policy that sees every drone at once. The team gets one shared, noisy clue about where the goal pads are, and the pads are a common pool: any drone may land on any pad that a teammate has not already claimed. Land every drone cleanly, fast, and without hitting the world or each other. The family is active and carries **0.20** of subnet emissions.

## Model I/O

The `dynamic` leading axis is the drone count for the current seed (2–8). Your graph receives all drones' observations stacked on axis 0 and must return one action row per drone, every control step.

|---|---|---|---|

### Action: float32 tensor, shape (dynamic, 5)

| # | Component | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `dir_x` | -1 | 1 |
| 1 | `dir_y` | -1 | 1 |
| 2 | `dir_z` | -1 | 1 |
| 3 | `speed` | 0 | 1 |
| 4 | `yaw` | -1 | 1 |

### Action semantics

Actions are velocity commands: the commanded velocity is `3.0 m/s × |speed| × normalize(dir_xyz)`, and `yaw × π` is an absolute target yaw in radians, rate-limited to 3.141 rad/s. A DSLPID controller converts the command into rotor RPMs on a CF2X Crazyflie (0.027 kg, 0.12 m hull radius). The runner canonicalizes every action before it reaches the sim: components are clipped to the contract bounds and quantized to a fixed step so every validator computes the identical value. A NaN/inf or wrong-shaped action is an artifact fault that fails the seed (`MG_OUTPUT_NONFINITE` / `MG_OUTPUT_CONTRACT`).

### Depth camera

Forward-facing, mounted 0.13 m ahead of and 0.05 m above the drone center. FOV is 90° ± 2°, randomized per seed. Rendered at 128 × 128 with PyBullet's TINY_RENDERER, far plane 30 m; the buffer is converted to meters, clipped to [0.5, 20] m, and normalized as `(d − 0.5) / 19.5`, so 1.0 means "20 m or nothing". No RGB channel in this family.

## State vector layout

Each drone's `state` row is 190 floats:

| Index | Channel | Size | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | `position_xyz` | 3 | World position, meters |
| 3–5 | `orientation_rpy` | 3 | Roll / pitch / yaw, radians |
| 6–8 | `linear_velocity_xyz` | 3 | m/s |
| 9–11 | `angular_velocity_xyz` | 3 | rad/s |
| 12–136 | `action_history` | 125 | The drone's own last 25 actions × 5 values, oldest → newest, zeroed at reset |
| 137 | `altitude_norm` | 1 | Downward ray distance ÷ 20 m, in [0, 1]; reads 1.0 when nothing is hit within 20 m |
| 138–140 | `goal_offset_xyz` | 3 | Shared noisy search-clue center − drone position, meters |
| 141–189 | `teammate_rel_state` | 49 | 7 fixed neighbour slots × 7 floats |

Kinematic channels are unbounded (`Box(-inf, inf)`).

### The search clue

`goal_offset_xyz` is **not** a per-drone goal. It points at one shared clue, computed once per seed: the mean of all goal pads, plus per-axis XY noise drawn uniformly from ±R with **R itself drawn uniformly from 5–20 m per seed** (capped so the seed stays completable within the horizon), plus Z noise of ±5 m (clamped to z ≥ 0). Every drone sees the same clue for the whole episode. Miners never receive pad coordinates: the pads must be found visually with the depth camera.

### Teammate slots

`teammate_rel_state` packs the other drones into 7 slots of 7 floats each: `[rel_pos x, y, z (m), rel_vel x, y, z (m/s), present]`. Slots are sorted nearest-first by 3D distance (ties broken by drone index); with fewer than 8 drones the unused slots are zero-filled with `present = 0.0`. Frozen teammates (already landed or crashed) still appear: they are real objects in the world.

## Maps

Five environment types: **city, open, mountain, village, forest**. Warehouse is excluded from this family. Moving platforms never occur here, even though the underlying template slots carry the flag: it is forced off for every family except single-drone Autopilot.

The full benchmark is 1,100 seeds. Seeds are generated from two templates cycled over the seed range: the first 300 seed indices use the 41-slot screening template, the remaining 800 use the 84-slot benchmark template (the Autopilot templates minus their warehouse slots). Drone count cycles `2 + (slot % 7)` across slots, so every count from 2 to 8 appears; seeds generated outside a template draw the count uniformly from 2–8.

Start → goal distance and goal-height bands per type (in the benchmark template each type's range is split into 3 distance bands; screening slots use the full range):

| Type | Benchmark distance (m) | Benchmark goal height (m) | Screening distance (m) | Screening goal height (m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| City | 22–45 | 0.2–1.0 | 22–40 | 0.3–0.8 |
| Open | 28–72 | 4.0–14.0 | 28–65 | 5–12 |
| Mountain | 65–100 | n/a | 65–95 | n/a |
| Village | 28–56 | n/a | 28–50 | n/a |
| Forest | 22–45 | 0.2–3.0 | 22–40 | 0.5–2.0 |

### Pad placement

Each seed lays out `n` start pads and `n` goal pads with a dedicated RNG (`seed + 313131`). Every pad is rejection-sampled (up to 100 attempts) to keep at least **4.0 m XY spacing** from every previously placed pad, starts and goals alike. Each goal is drawn from its own start within the slot's distance band.

Start pad heights: city, open, and forest starts sit on a spawned platform with a randomized surface height of **0.2–10 m**; mountain starts on the terrain surface; village starts at ground level. The drone spawns 0.121 m above its pad surface.

One shared map is built from the task's `map_seed`; then each drone `i` gets its start and goal platforms placed with seed `map_seed + i × 101`, nudged as needed to clear obstacles (the adjusted positions are what scoring uses). On open maps the ground plane is dropped to z = −1000 and made invisible: there is no floor to cheat off.

## Episode rules

| | |
|---|---|
| Sim / control rate | 50 Hz (`sim_dt` = 1/50 s), 1 physics substep per control step |
| Horizon | 60 s = 3,000 control steps, every map type |
| Drone | CF2X Crazyflie, 0.027 kg, 0.12 m hull radius |
| Episode end | When **every** drone is resolved (landed or failed); at 60 s all unresolved drones are stamped `TIMEOUT` |

### Shared pad pool

Goal platforms are a single-use common pool: any drone may land on **any** goal platform no teammate has claimed yet. A stable landing claims that platform; claimed platforms become exempt bodies: touching one afterwards is neither a landing nor a collision.

### Per-drone success

A drone succeeds by holding continuous stable contact with an unclaimed goal platform for **0.5 s**, with:

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| Vertical speed | \|vz\| ≤ 0.5 m/s |
| Horizontal speed | ≤ 0.6 m/s |
| Tilt | \|roll\| and \|pitch\| ≤ 0.26 rad |

On success the drone's `t_to_goal` is recorded and it is frozen in place.

### Per-drone failure

| Reason | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `OBSTACLE_COLLISION` | Any contact with normal force > 0.01 N against a non-exempt body. **Live teammates are not exempt: drone-drone contact is a collision.** |
| `TILT` | \|roll\| or \|pitch\| > 1.047 rad (~60°) |
| `TIMEOUT` | Still unresolved at 60 s |

A failed drone is frozen where it is, its mass set to 0, and it remains in the world as a static obstacle for the rest of the team. The physics loop skips frozen drones (no motor forces), but they still block paths and pads.

### Safety clearance

Each drone's minimum clearance is tracked every step within a 1.0 m search box, starting at 1.0. Live teammates and platform bodies are **excluded**: flying close to a teammate never lowers the safety term, only real impacts do. A collided drone's clearance is forced to 0.0.

## Scoring

The seed's score is the **arithmetic mean of the per-drone scores**. A drone that lands cleanly (no collision beforehand) scores:

```
per_drone = clamp( 0.45 · success + 0.45 · time_factor + 0.10 · safety_factor )   ∈ [0, 1]
```

with `success = 1.0` on that path. A drone that does not land cleanly (including one that lands after colliding) scores the participation reward **0.01** (0.0 if it never flew). An evaluation error or an illegitimate model scores 0.0 outright.

### Time factor

```
time_factor = 1.0                                   if t ≤ target_time
            = clamp(1 − (t − target_time) / (60 − target_time))   otherwise
```

`t` is the drone's `t_to_goal` on success, else the full 60 s horizon. The per-drone `target_time` allows a straight-line flight plus congestion slack:

```
target_time = 1.06 × (distance / 3.0 m/s) + 1.0 s × (n_drones − 1)
```

where `distance` is the 3D straight-line distance from that drone's (adjusted) start to the goal pad nearest its start by horizontal distance. Every teammate adds one second of slack, regardless of where it flies.

### Safety factor

```
safety_factor = 1.0    if min_clearance ≥ 1.0 m   (0.6 m on forest maps)
              = 0.0    if min_clearance ≤ 0.2 m, or on collision
              linear in between
```

### Per-seed result

The seed's `ValidationResult` carries `score` (the mean), `success` (true only if **all** drones landed), the first non-`NONE` per-drone failure reason when the seed is not fully successful, and per-drone metrics (`per_drone_final_score`, `per_drone_success`, `per_drone_failure_reason`).

Submissions run the full 1,100-seed benchmark. A screening admission phase exists behind a backend constant (`SCREENING_ENABLED`, off by default); if it is ever switched on, this family's policy applies a 0.01 bootstrap threshold, a 0.015 minimum improvement over the champion (floor 0.005 as scores approach 1.0), and early-fail checkpoints at seeds 50/100/150 with thresholds 0.5/0.7/0.85. The 0.015 → 0.005 improvement floor also gates benchmark crowning.

## Runtime limits

Your model runs in Docker as a Cap'n Proto RPC server; the validator calls `reset()` between seeds and `act(obs)` once per 50 Hz step. This family uses the default timing budget:

| Call | Budget |
|---|---|
| `ping` | 2.0 s |
| `reset()` | 5.0 s |
| First `act()` of a seed | 2.0 s guaranteed compute (3.0 ref-sec hard cap) |
| Every later `act()` | 0.6 s baseline-equivalent guaranteed compute (2.0 ref-sec + measured RPC overhead + 0.05 s margin hard cap) |

A timed-out step substitutes a zero action and counts a strike; **15 strikes** or **3 hard-cap timeouts** fail the seed with score 0.

The pre-eval smoke test instantiates your controller and calls `reset()` + `act()` at **both ends of the drone range (n = 2 and n = 8)**, with synthetic observations; the returned action must be shape `(n, 5)`, finite, and within bounds. Make sure your policy handles the dynamic axis before submitting.

`requirements.txt` is limited to the Docker pip whitelist: torch/torchvision/torchaudio, onnx/onnxruntime(-gpu), stable-baselines3, sb3-contrib, gymnasium/gym, swarm-bullet3, swarm-drone-gym, numpy, scipy, scikit-learn, opencv-python(-headless), pillow, imageio, matplotlib, pyyaml, tqdm, einops, tensorboard, h5py, msgpack.

swarm benchmark --model Submission/submission.zip --family-id cf_swarm_autopilot --workers 4

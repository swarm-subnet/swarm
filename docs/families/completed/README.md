# Completed families

These challenge families are completed: they take no submissions, are not scored and pay nothing. Their guides stay here for reference, and every past champion stays published in the [champions repository](https://github.com/swarm-subnet/swarm-champions).

| Family | ID | Maps it ran on | Share it carried | Why it closed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Interceptor](interceptor.md) | `cf_interceptor` | Open | 30% | Its champion reached the solve threshold; the winning solution is open source |
| [Autopilot](autopilot.md) | `cf_autopilot` | City, Open, Mountain, Village, Warehouse, Forest | 15% | Swarm Sentinel took its place |
| [Swarm Autopilot](swarm_autopilot.md) | `cf_swarm_autopilot` | City, Open, Mountain, Village, Forest | 20% | Swarm Sentinel took its place |
| [Office Interceptor](interceptor_office.md) | `cf_interceptor_office` | Office (challenge type 7) | 30% | Swarm Sentinel took its place |

Swarm Sentinel's slice of emissions is the sum of the three it replaced. The office map (challenge type 7) was used only by Office Interceptor, so it is completed too; the other maps live on in the rescue families.

The live families are [Swarm Sentinel](../solar_patrol.md), [Swarm Search and Rescue](../swarm_sar.md) and [Search and Rescue](../search_and_rescue.md).

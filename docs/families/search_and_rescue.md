# Search and Rescue

Find a human on the ground with nothing but a depth camera, a noisy clue, and 40 colour snapshots, then hover overhead without ever touching them.

---

A victim is down somewhere in the world. Your drone lifts off from a start pad with a rough 2D pointer toward the search area: the victim's true position lies anywhere within a 30 m circle around that point. The drone must fly out, sweep the area, locate the victim, and hold a steady confirmation hover 2–4 m above them for 2 continuous seconds. Getting within 0.8 m of the victim ends the mission in failure: this is a rescue, not a delivery.

This family runs on the **private track**. Submit with `--family_id cf_search_and_rescue --artifact ./submission.zip` (see [miner.md](../miner.md#submit-a-private-family-search-and-rescue-swarm-sar)), not a public repo: the sha256 is committed on-chain and the bytes go to the backend. The artifact and its source stay private; only trusted validators fetch it. Scores and the leaderboard stay public like any other family.

### Action semantics

The base control mode is velocity control (`VEL`): commanded velocity = `3.0 m/s × |speed| × unit(dir_xyz)`. `yaw` maps to an absolute target heading of `yaw × π` rad, slewed at most 3.141 rad/s per control step. Position hold at the current position is handled by the onboard DSLPID controller. SAR appends the 6th component, `rgb_request`: set it above 0.5 to receive a colour frame (see [Cameras](#cameras)).

The runner canonicalizes every action before it reaches the sim: components are clipped to the contract bounds and quantized to a fixed step so every validator computes the identical value. A NaN/inf or wrong-shaped action is an artifact fault that fails the seed (`MG_OUTPUT_NONFINITE` / `MG_OUTPUT_CONTRACT`).

## State vector layout

165 floats at the production 50 Hz control rate. Read the length dynamically if you can: it is `12 + (ctrl_freq/2 × 6) + 3` and would shift if the control rate ever changed.

| Index | Channel | Meaning | Units / frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | `position_xyz` | Drone position | m, world frame |
| 3–5 | `orientation_rpy` | Roll, pitch, yaw | rad |
| 6–8 | `linear_velocity_xyz` | Linear velocity | m/s, world frame |
| 9–11 | `angular_velocity_xyz` | Angular velocity | rad/s |
| 12–161 | `action_history` | Last 25 raw 6-dim actions, concatenated oldest-first; zeroed on reset | as passed to `act` |
| 162 | `altitude_norm` | Downward ray distance ÷ 20 m; reads 1.0 when nothing is within 20 m below | [0, 1] |
| 163–164 | `search_clue_offset` | `(search_centre − drone_pos)` in x, y | m, world frame |

The contract labels the last channel `search_clue_offset_xyz`, but it carries exactly **two** values, x and y, no z. The clue points at the **search centre**, not the victim: the victim's true XY lies somewhere within **30 m** of that point (see [Episode rules](#episode-rules) for how the offset is sampled). Steering the offset to zero puts you at the centre of the search circle; from there it is a perception problem.

## Cameras

Both cameras share one pose: forward-facing, mounted 0.13 m ahead of and 0.05 m above the drone centre (body frame), looking 20 m ahead along body +X. FOV is 90° with a ±2° per-seed randomization. Rendering is PyBullet's TinyRenderer with shadows off and a seeded light direction.

**Depth**: always on, 256×256×1. Metric depth is clipped to [0.5 m, 30 m] and normalized as `(d − 0.5) / 29.5`. The 30 m range (vs the 20 m default in the autopilot families) is your primary search sensor.

**RGB**: on demand only, 256×256×3, raw pixel values ÷ 255. The `rgb` slot is **all zeros** unless your action set `rgb_request > 0.5` that step, and each drone has a hard budget of **40 requests per episode**: spend them confirming candidate sightings, not scanning. A request in the action returned at step *t* produces the frame in the observation returned by that same step, the very next `obs` your policy sees. The slot re-zeroes the following step unless you request again.

## Maps

SAR runs on **all six** environment types: City, Open, Mountain, Village, Warehouse, Forest. On Open maps, the default ground plane is dropped away and replaced by SAR-generated terrain; on every SAR map the plane is collision-exempt.

Benchmark seeds are built from two templates. Each table's distance column is the per-type overall straight-line start→goal range: in the benchmark template that range is split into three equal bands across the type's slots, while in the screening template every slot draws from the full listed range.

**Benchmark template**: 100 interleaved slots, three distance bands per type, no moving platforms (seed indices 300+):

| Type | Slots | Distance band |
|---|---|---|
| City | 17 | 15–28 m |
| Open | 17 | 14–22 m |
| Mountain | 17 | 30–60 m |
| Village | 17 | 25–50 m |
| Warehouse | 16 | 10–25 m |
| Forest | 16 | 15–31 m |

**Screening template**: 50 interleaved slots, used for seed indices 0–299 of the 1,100 even though screening itself is disabled:

| Type | Slots | Distance band |
|---|---|---|
| City | 8 | 15–25 m |
| Open | 8 | 14–20 m |
| Mountain | 8 | 30–55 m |
| Village | 9 | 25–45 m |
| Warehouse | 9 | 10–22 m |
| Forest | 8 | 15–28 m |

Every epoch runs 1,100 seeds: indices below 300 use the screening template, the remaining 800 use the benchmark template. Seeds rotate every 7 days (Monday 16:00 UTC).

## Episode rules

### Start

The drone spawns level on a physical start pad erected at the task's start XY on the resolved surface (in warehouses: on the floor, nudged clear of racks), 0.121 m above the pad top. Your own pad is collision-exempt.

### Victim and clue

The victim is a humanoid mannequin mesh, chosen per seed (scale 0.6 on City/Village, 1.0 elsewhere), standing or lying depending on local slope. Placement is deterministic rejection sampling: up to 50 attempts, with candidate XY drawn uniformly within a per-type bound (City/Village 25 m, Open/Mountain 30 m, Warehouse 12 m, Forest 20 m), for a spot with a supported surface, terrain slope ≤ 22°, a clear 5 m hover column above, a clear 0.8 m no-touch box, and at most **80 m** from the start (the nearest over-distance candidate is used as a fallback). If sampling exhausts, the episode reports `SPAWN_FAILURE`.

The **search centre** you see in the state clue is the victim's XY plus a uniform-in-disk offset of radius 30 m (`r = 30·√u`, uniform angle), drawn from its own seeded RNG. Reaching the clue point is the start of the search, not the end.

### Success: CONFIRMED

All four conditions must hold **simultaneously and continuously for 2.0 s** (the dwell timer resets to zero the instant any condition drops):

| Condition | Threshold |
|---|---|
| 3D distance to victim centre | ≥ 0.8 m (outside the no-touch sphere) |
| Horizontal distance to victim centre | ≤ 2.0 m |
| Speed | ≤ 1.0 m/s |
| Height above the victim's bounding-box top | 2.0–4.0 m |

Once the predicate is active, the horizontal-distance, speed, and hover-band bounds each get 0.1 of hysteresis slack (0.1 m / 0.1 m·s⁻¹), so a centimetre of drift will not reset your dwell. The 0.8 m no-touch sphere never loosens: drifting inside it both drops the predicate and terminates the episode.

### Failure

| Reason | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `NO_TOUCH_SPHERE` | 3D distance to victim centre drops below 0.8 m: immediate termination |
| `OBSTACLE_COLLISION` | Contact force > 0.01 N against any non-exempt body; touching the victim's body counts as an obstacle hit |
| `TILT` | \|roll\| or \|pitch\| exceeds 1.047 rad (~60°) |
| `TIMEOUT` | 60 s horizon reached without a confirm |
| `INFEASIBLE` | Early stop: the time left cannot cover flying straight at 3.0 m/s to the search circle's edge plus the remaining dwell |
| `SPAWN_FAILURE` | Victim placement sampling exhausted |

The infeasible check truncates when `time_left < max(0, horiz_dist_to_search_centre − 30) / 3.0 + remaining_dwell`: no point simulating an episode you can no longer win.

## Scoring

```
score = clamp01(0.45 × success + 0.45 × time + 0.10 × safety)
```

The full stack only pays on a CONFIRMED hover. Any non-success outcome (`OBSTACLE_COLLISION`, `NO_TOUCH_SPHERE`, `TILT`, `TIMEOUT`, `INFEASIBLE`, `SPAWN_FAILURE`) scores the flat **0.01** participation reward. A confirm with a collision on record also collapses to 0.01: a crash voids the mission. Evaluator errors and non-legitimate models score 0.0.

| Term | Weight | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Success** | 0.45 | 1.0 on CONFIRMED, else 0.0 |
| **Time** | 0.45 | 1.0 if confirmed within the target time; decays linearly to 0.0 at the 60 s horizon |
| **Safety** | 0.10 | From the minimum clearance held all episode: 1.0 at ≥ 1.0 m (Forest: ≥ 0.6 m), 0.0 at ≤ 0.2 m, linear between |

The time target budgets a realistic search, not a beeline:

```
target_time = 1.03 × ( dist(start, search_centre) / 3.0        # transit at speed limit
                     + 0.70 × π × 30² / (24 × 3.0)              # partial sweep of the circle, ≈ 27.5 s
                     + 2.0 )                                    # the dwell
```

Clearance tracking for the safety term ignores the victim's body, your own start pad, and the safety patch on the victim's support surface: hovering close over the victim does not cost safety.

For local training, `env.step` returns the per-step **change** in this same rollout score, so reward shaping in the gym matches benchmark scoring exactly.

## Runtime limits

Models run inside a sandboxed Docker container (6 GB RAM, 2 CPUs) as a Cap'n Proto RPC server; `requirements.txt` is restricted to the [whitelist](../miner.md#docker-whitelist) and the uncompressed zip is capped at 50 MiB.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Per-step `act` timeout | 0.5 s (first step: 2.0 s) |
| `reset` timeout | 5.0 s |
| Strikes per seed | 15 |
| Calibrated compute budget | 0.5 baseline-equivalent s per `act`, hard cap 1.25 ref-s |

Compute is hardware-fair: a calibration pass converts your wall-clock into baseline-equivalent seconds, so a fast validator host does not buy you a bigger model.

New submissions go straight to the full 1,100-seed benchmark. A champion-gated 300-seed screening stage exists in the backend behind the `SCREENING_ENABLED` constant, but it is off. Its policy thresholds only matter if operators enable it.

## Local testing

Package your model against this family's contract, then benchmark it locally with the engine's family flag:

```bash
swarm model package --source ./my_model --family-id cf_search_and_rescue

python -m swarm.benchmark.engine \
  --model submission.zip \
  --family-id cf_search_and_rescue \
  --seeds-per-group 3 --workers 4
```

`--seeds-per-group` controls seeds per environment type; validators run the full 1,100. The plain `swarm benchmark` subcommand does not forward a family flag and runs the default family (`cf_autopilot`). Use the engine module directly for SAR.
